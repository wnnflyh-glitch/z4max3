# ⚖️ Tax System

### Overview

The **Tax System** in Zone4 is a mechanism that applies fees to item transactions between players (Player-to-Player Trading) within the **Square / Marketplace**.

When players set up a shop or trade items, the system will deduct a tax based on a specified percentage before the payment is transferred to the seller.

**Purpose of the Tax System:**

* Control the amount of currency in the game
* Maintain a balanced in-game economy

***

### **Tax Structure**

In Zone4, item trading can be conducted through the **Player Shop** or the **Square Market**, each with different tax rates depending on the shop type.

**P2P Trading (Player-to-Player)**

P2P Trading refers to direct item trading between players within the game. This typically occurs through:

* **Player Trade**\
  Players can exchange certain items or **ZEN** with other players.

***

**P2P Tax System**\
In P2P trading, the system deducts a tax from the sale price before transferring the remaining amount to the seller.

***

**Tax Calculation**

**Example:**

* Item Price: **100,000 ZEN**
* Tax Rate: **10%**

**Calculation:**

* Tax = 100,000 × 10%
* Tax = **10,000**

**Amount received by the seller:**

* **90,000 ZEN**
