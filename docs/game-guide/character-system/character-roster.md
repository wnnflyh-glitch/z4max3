# Character Roster

## **Overview**

In the current Zone4 system, there are **6 main characters (Base Characters)** that players can choose as their in-game avatars. These characters are members of a group of fighters associated with the **Saints Association** and the **FFC (Fight Festival Championship)**, which serves as the central focus of the game’s storyline.



### 1. Wells

| Age        | 25                     |
| Gender     | Male                   |
| Occupation | Toothbrush Salesperson |
| Hobby      | Beat Boxing            |
| Blood Type | O                      |

#### **Character Background**

Wells is a strong-built young man who appears like a thug, but is actually kind-hearted and not very fond of fighting. One day, he saved Nicky from danger and immediately fell in love with her. After learning that Nicky prefers strong men, he began training in martial arts and joined the Saints Association to prove himself.

**Character Identity**

* Main character of the game
* Central figure in the storyline
* Well-balanced in combat

#### **Character Background**

Ray is a cold and disciplined athlete, and a disciple of the legendary fighter Roddy Heart. After his master disappeared, Ray set out to carry on Roddy’s dream of restoring the Saints Association and bringing prestige back to the FFC.

**Character Identity**

* A fighter with strong leadership qualities
* Calm and composed personality
* Has a special relationship with Sera

#### **Character Background**

Joony is the son of Davark, a genius fighter who once stood alone against the By-X organization. Growing up under the pressure of his father’s legacy, Joony strives to prove that he can become a fighter even greater than the legend himself.

**Character Identity**

* A genius teenage fighter
* Highly ambitious
* Has a conflicted relationship with his father

#### **Character Background**

Nicky is a detective assigned to investigate the secrets behind the FFC that took place four years ago. During her investigation, her identity was exposed, putting her in danger. #### **Character Background**

* A key character who connects the relationships between multiple characters
