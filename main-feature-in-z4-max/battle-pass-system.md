# 🔢 Battle Pass System

## Overview

The **Battle Pass (BP)** is a seasonal progression system in Zone4 that provides players with additional reward paths through in-game activities. It is divided into two tracks:

* **Battle Pass Quest (BP Free Line)**
* **Battle Pass Premium (BP Premium Line)**

Players can level up their Battle Pass to earn more rewards as the season progresses. In **Season 1**, the maximum level is **Level 50**.

The Battle Pass system enhances in-game activities, increases item circulation, encourages players to complete daily and weekly quests, and helps maintain a balanced in-game economy.

***

**Battle Pass Quest (BP Quest)**

* **Price:** 14,900 DE
* Progress by completing **Daily Quests** and **Weekly Quests**
* Earn items used to level up the Battle Pass through **BP Quest Fragments → BP Crystals**
* Suitable for **Free-to-Play players** and regular active players

### Battle Pass Quest System

The **Battle Pass (BP)** system in Zone4 is designed to allow players to **progress their Battle Pass level daily** through regular gameplay, without needing to manually accept quests in advance.

**Core Concept: “Auto-Tracking Quest”**\
Players do not need to accept any quests. When conditions are met, the system automatically tracks progress and sends **BP Fragments** directly to the player’s **Mail**.

BP Fragments can then be exchanged for **BP Crystals** at **NPC Franklin**, which are used to **level up the player’s Battle Pass**.

***

* **PvE**

### NPC Franklin – Exchange Flow

**Exchange Flow**

* Talk to **NPC Franklin**
* Players bring **BP Quest Fragments**
* Exchange them for **BP Crystals**
* Use BP Crystals to increase the **Battle Pass Quest level**

**BP Crystals** are the key resource for progressing the BP Quest system.
