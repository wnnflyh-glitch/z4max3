# ⚡ Stamina System

## Overview

The **Stamina System** is a core mechanic used to control how often players can participate in activities in Zone4 Max Combo each day. Its purpose is to maintain gameplay balance and regulate the rate at which players earn rewards and in-game resources.

Stamina, or Energy, serves as the primary resource required to enter certain game modes such as Arcade Mode, Dungeon, and PvP Mode. Players must spend Energy to participate in these activities, and once their Energy is depleted, they will no longer be able to join Energy-based activities until it is reset or replenished.

This system encourages players to plan their daily gameplay efficiently while helping maintain a balanced in-game economy.

**Daily Stamina Reset**

Players’ Stamina will reset daily at a fixed time, allowing everyone to receive fresh Energy for the next day’s gameplay.

**Reset Details:**

* Stamina resets every day at **06:00 (Server Time)**
* At the reset time, Stamina will be restored up to each character’s **Max Stamina**
* Max Stamina is determined by the player’s **level**

**Important Rules:**

* Any unused Stamina will <mark style="color:$danger;">not carry over to the next day</mark>
* If a player is still online at the reset time (06:00), the Stamina reset will only take effect after one of the following actions:
  * Logging out of the game
  * Changing and switch Channels
  * Entering Arcade Mode or other activities

***

**Arcade Mode / Dungeon**\
All players are required to spend Energy to enter Arcade Mode or Dungeon.

**Energy Usage Details:**

* Consumes **4 Energy per run**
* Arcade and Dungeon are the main activities for:
  * Gaining EXP
  * Obtaining items
  * Earning rewards from monsters

The higher Energy cost compared to PvP is designed to regulate the rate of resource farming within the game.

***

**PvP Mode**\
Players can participate in PvP Mode to compete against other players.

**Energy Usage Details:**

* Consumes **1 Energy per match**

The lower Energy cost encourages players to engage in PvP more frequently.

***

## Max Stamina System

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (108).png" alt="" width="563"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

The maximum amount of Stamina a player can have each day depends on their character’s level. As players level up:

* Max Stamina increases
* The number of activities they can participate in each day also increases

This system creates a progression incentive, encouraging players to continuously develop their characters.

\
**Example concept for value scaling:**

| Player Level | Max Stamina |
| ------------ | ----------- |
| Level 1–19   | 100         |
| Level 19–39  | 200         |
| Level 40–59  | 300         |

### **Energy Acquisition**

In addition to the daily reset, players can obtain extra Energy through various in-game activities:

***

**1. DE Shop**\
Players can purchase additional Energy from the in-game DE Shop. This allows players to:

* Participate in more activities
* Farm additional resources
* Join more special events

***

**2. Daily / Weekly Missions**\
Players can earn Energy as rewards from:

* Daily Missions
* Weekly Missions

***

**3. In-game Events**\
Energy can also be obtained from special in-game events, such as:

* Event Login Rewards
* Special Event Missions
* Seasonal Events
