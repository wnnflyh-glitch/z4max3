# How to register a ZONE4 MAX Combo Account

A ZONE4 MAX Combo Account gives you access to our ecosystem and do things like:

* Play ZONE4 MAX Combo and related Ecosystem

Follow these steps to create your ZONE4 MAX Combo Account:

1. Go to the Maxion Account.
2. Click on \[Continue with Google], the system will automatically create a new Maxion account for you if your email doesn't have an accoun&#x74;**.**
3. In the ZONE4 MAX Combo section, click \[Register game account]
4. Enter your MAX Combo Username and password.
5. Click **Register**.

After successfully registered a ZONE4 MAX Combo Account, you can directly use it to play the game.
