# 🎛️ Costume Mix System

## Overview

The **Costume Mix System** in Zone4 allows players to combine **2 Costume Items** through **NPC Dr. K** using a special item called the **Costume Cube** to create a new item with a **randomized result**.

This system is designed to:

* Recycle unwanted Costume items
* Give players a chance to obtain **Special Costumes** from the mix

Costume Mix enables players to transform their existing fashion items into new ones, without needing to purchase new items from the shop.

## Costume Mix Result

### Success

When the mix process is successful, the system will:

* Remove both original Costume items
* Add the new item to the Inventory

### Failure

If the mix fails

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/Costume Mix.gif" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

the result will be:

* No item is obtained
* One Sub Item is destroyed
